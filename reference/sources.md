# Sources

Status *unverified* means the work and its position are cited from general
knowledge, and locators other than ancient book/section numbers still need
checking against a copy. Change it to *verified* in `data/sources.yaml` once
checked.

## Ancient sources

- <a id="clement-1"></a>**1 Clement**: *1 Clement* (letter of the church of Rome to Corinth, usually dated c. AD 95–96). *(unverified)*
- <a id="maccabees-1"></a>**1 Maccabees**: *1 Maccabees* (late 2nd century BC): the desecration of the temple by Antiochus IV (15 Kislev 167 BC, 1:54) and its rededication (25 Kislev 164 BC, 4:52). *(unverified)*
- <a id="dio"></a>**Cassius Dio, Roman History**: Cassius Dio, *Roman History* (early 3rd century AD). Loeb Classical Library. *(unverified)*
- <a id="dead-sea-scrolls"></a>**Dead Sea Scrolls**: Biblical manuscripts from Qumran (c. 250 BC – AD 68), e.g. the Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsaᵃ, c. 125 BC) and copies of Daniel from the late 2nd century BC; they set the latest possible date for each book's text. *(unverified)*
- <a id="elephantine-papyri"></a>**Elephantine papyri**: Aramaic papyri from the Jewish garrison at Elephantine, Egypt (5th century BC); the petition to Bagohi of 407 BC names the high priest Johanan and the sons of Sanballat. Translation in ANET 491–492. *(unverified)*
- <a id="eusebius-he"></a>**Eusebius, Church History**: Eusebius of Caesarea, *Ecclesiastical History* (early 4th century AD). *(unverified)*
- <a id="irenaeus"></a>**Irenaeus, Against Heresies**: Irenaeus of Lyon, *Against Heresies* (c. AD 180). *(unverified)*
- <a id="josephus-apion"></a>**Josephus, Against Apion**: Flavius Josephus, *Against Apion* (c. AD 95); 1.37–43 on the 22 books of the Jewish scriptures, written from Moses to Artaxerxes. *(unverified)*
- <a id="josephus-ant"></a>**Josephus, Antiquities**: Flavius Josephus, *Jewish Antiquities* (c. AD 93–94). Greek text and translation: Loeb Classical Library; public-domain translation by William Whiston (1737). *(unverified)*
- <a id="josephus-war"></a>**Josephus, Jewish War**: Flavius Josephus, *The Jewish War* (c. AD 75–79). Loeb Classical Library; Whiston translation. *(unverified)*
- <a id="orosius"></a>**Orosius, History**: Paulus Orosius, *Seven Books of History against the Pagans* (c. AD 417). *(unverified)*
- <a id="seder-olam"></a>**Seder Olam Rabbah**: *Seder Olam Rabbah* (rabbinic chronology attributed to Yose ben Halafta, 2nd century AD), the basis of the traditional Jewish era from creation. *(unverified)*
- <a id="sirach"></a>**Sirach**: *Wisdom of Ben Sira* (Sirach), c. 180 BC, translated by his grandson c. 132 BC; chs. 44–50 survey Israel's heroes and books, including 'the Twelve Prophets' (49:10). *(unverified)*
- <a id="suetonius-claud"></a>**Suetonius, Claudius**: Suetonius, *Lives of the Caesars*, 'Claudius' (c. AD 121). Loeb Classical Library. *(unverified)*
- <a id="tacitus-ann"></a>**Tacitus, Annals**: Cornelius Tacitus, *Annals* (c. AD 116–120). Loeb Classical Library. *(unverified)*
- <a id="bava-batra"></a>**Talmud, Bava Batra**: Babylonian Talmud, *Bava Batra* 14b–15a (compiled c. AD 500, using earlier traditions): the traditional authors and order of the books of the Hebrew Bible. *(unverified)*

## Inscriptions

- <a id="eponym-canon"></a>**Assyrian eponym list**: The Assyrian eponym (limmu) lists, which name each year after an official and record a solar eclipse in the eponymy of Bur-Saggile, astronomically fixed to 15 June 763 BC. This fixes Assyrian, and through synchronisms Israelite, chronology. *(unverified)*
- <a id="babylonian-chronicle"></a>**Babylonian Chronicle**: Babylonian Chronicles, especially ABC 5 (the early years of Nebuchadnezzar II): Carchemish (605 BC) and the capture of Jerusalem on 2 Adar of year 7 (15/16 March 597 BC). Edition: Grayson 1975. *(unverified)*
- <a id="black-obelisk"></a>**Black Obelisk**: Black Obelisk of Shalmaneser III, British Museum: tribute of 'Jehu son of Omri' in Shalmaneser's 18th year (841 BC). Translation in ANET 280–281. *(unverified)*
- <a id="cyrus-cylinder"></a>**Cyrus Cylinder**: Cyrus Cylinder (539 BC or soon after), British Museum: Cyrus's capture of Babylon and his return of deported peoples and their gods. Translation in ANET 315–316. *(unverified)*
- <a id="gallio-inscription"></a>**Delphi (Gallio) inscription**: Letter of Claudius to Delphi naming L. Junius Gallio as proconsul of Achaia, dated by Claudius's 26th imperial acclamation (AD 52). *Fouilles de Delphes* III.4 no. 286; SIG³ 801D. *(unverified)*
- <a id="kurkh-monolith"></a>**Kurkh Monolith**: Kurkh Monolith of Shalmaneser III, British Museum: the battle of Qarqar (853 BC), naming 'Ahab the Israelite' among the coalition. Translation in ANET 278–279. *(unverified)*
- <a id="merneptah-stele"></a>**Merneptah Stele**: Victory stele of Pharaoh Merneptah, year 5 (c. 1208 BC), Cairo Museum; the earliest mention of 'Israel' outside the Bible, as a people in Canaan. Translation in ANET 376–378. *(unverified)*
- <a id="mesha-stele"></a>**Mesha Stele**: Mesha Stele (Moabite Stone), c. 840 BC, Louvre; King Mesha of Moab on Omri's dynasty and his revolt (cf. 2 Kings 3). Translation in ANET 320–321. *(unverified)*
- <a id="sennacherib-annals"></a>**Sennacherib's annals**: Annals of Sennacherib (Taylor, Oriental Institute and Jerusalem prisms) on the campaign of 701 BC: Hezekiah shut up in Jerusalem 'like a bird in a cage'. Translation in ANET 287–288. *(unverified)*
- <a id="shoshenq-relief"></a>**Shoshenq I relief**: Shoshenq I's (biblical Shishak's) list of conquered towns in Canaan on the Bubastite Portal at Karnak, c. 925 BC. *(unverified)*
- <a id="tel-dan"></a>**Tel Dan inscription**: Aramaic victory stele from Tel Dan, 9th century BC, naming a king of 'the house of David'. A. Biran and J. Naveh, *Israel Exploration Journal* 43 (1993): 81–98 and 45 (1995): 1–18. *(unverified)*
